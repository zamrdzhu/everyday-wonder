One sentence on a small moment of wonder in everyday life, written for make.fun's "Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line" bounty: a single sentence, 200 characters at most, grounded in a real and specific morning.

## The line

> At 6 a.m. the kettle steam drew one perfect spiral — the whole universe, rehearsing in my kitchen, before the toast popped.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The surprise is in the angle: a wisp of kettle steam becomes the whole universe rehearsing, and the kitchen is its stage. It lands in one read as a single clear sentence, grounded in something real — 6 a.m., a kettle, toast — with no filler and no worn phrase. The toast popping is the small, ordinary snap back to earth.
